Inflammatory and Paediatric Practice

Simulated teaching cases · clinician review required

Childhood Itch

A child has recurrent itch and poorly demarcated dry patches in the elbow folds. Which morphology and history make one diagnosis more likely?

Reveal reasoning

Atopic dermatitis. The combination of itch, a recurrent course and flexural eczema is supportive. Consider contact dermatitis or scabies if the exposure, distribution or household history points elsewhere.

Open reference record · Open source

Crusted Facial Lesions in a Child

A child develops shallow perinasal erosions with adherent golden crust. What would you describe before naming a diagnosis?

Reveal reasoning

Nonbullous impetigo is supported by the combination of superficial erosions and honey-coloured crust. Eczema can have secondary infection. Check for underlying itch and dermatitis before interpreting the crust in isolation.

Open reference record · Open source

Scaly Extensor Plaques

An adult has symmetric, sharply defined scaly plaques on both elbows and knees. Compare the border, distribution and scale with an eczematous plaque.

Reveal reasoning

Plaque psoriasis is supported by the sharp borders and symmetric extensor distribution. An annular plaque with an active edge or asymmetry would prompt reconsideration of fungal infection among the alternatives.

Open reference record · Open source

About Docutis

Who this preview is for, and what it is not.

What It Is

Docutis is an open-source, structured dermatology reference and education toolkit for physicians, medical trainees and other healthcare professionals. It organizes concise educational records, follow-up references, visual learning aids and a small quiz behind a transparent clinical-review status model. It is an educational and professional reference in active development (a public preview, not a finished release) and is not a substitute for individual care. Some assets are clinician reviewed; most content is still review required.

Current Status

Public preview. This dashboard shows current review counts for the library, cases, quiz, visuals and follow-up protocols. Physician review applies to a specific version of each item. Evidence Dashboard

Not validated clinical decision support. Content is educational draft material. It does not constitute clinician-approved recommendations for diagnosis, treatment or follow-up.

Limitations

  • Coverage is incomplete; many dermatology domains remain future work.
  • Product-specific dosing and jurisdiction-specific information are included only when supported by traceable current guidelines or regulatory product information and still require professional verification before clinical use.
  • A published review requires a current physician decision that matches the content version.
  • Always verify claims against current guidelines and professional judgment.
