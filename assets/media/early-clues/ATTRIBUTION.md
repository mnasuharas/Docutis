# Early-clue source-image acquisition

Ten new source-image records create ten additional comparisons (fifteen total).
They come from **four publications**, not ten independent publications or ten independently verified patients.
Six panels originate in the same XP cohort. Panel-to-patient independence is unknown.
Nine source labels are melanoma in situ; one is invasive melanoma with source Breslow 0.75 mm.
Source histopathology reports are not Docutis physician approval. All teaching remains clinician review required.

Each image was visually inspected before selecting a panel. Crops separate source panels;
no generated structures, retouching, or contrast enhancement was applied. Large panels were
proportionally reduced to at most 1300 pixels and saved as JPEG quality 95. Published arrows,
boxes and feature labels remain. These are guided exercises, not blinded diagnostic assessments.
The comparison controls are existing cases from other sources/devices/sites and are not matched controls.

`early-clue-records.json` records the direct source-image URL, DOI, panel, crop rectangle in original
pixels, original and output dimensions, output SHA-256, author attribution and image license.
The Huang image and its derivative retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; this asset is not relicensed under the code license.
Its use here is noncommercial education. Other new image derivatives retain CC BY 4.0.

Metadata checked: 2026-11-27. No treatment instructions were extracted from these sources.
