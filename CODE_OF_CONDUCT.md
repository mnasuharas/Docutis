# Code of conduct

Docutis welcomes respectful participation from clinicians, trainees, engineers, designers and other contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise and constructive.
- Discuss evidence and implementation decisions without personal attacks.
- Protect privacy; never post identifiable patient information.
- Disclose uncertainty and relevant conflicts of interest.
- Accept that clinical claims require reliable sources and appropriate review.
- Make accessibility and inclusive language part of the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, fabricated credentials or review attestations, and publication of confidential or patient-identifiable information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns through the repository’s available private maintainer or moderation channel; do not expose sensitive details publicly.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.
